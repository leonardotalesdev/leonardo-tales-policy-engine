# Contributing

External contributions remain **CLOSED** until separately authorized. Making source visible and accepting contributions are separate human decisions. This statement does not claim that a hosting platform technically prevents forks or pull requests.

Hakan Demirci is the sole maintainer. There is no automatic acceptance of issues, pull requests, or merges. Every accepted change and merge requires explicit human review and approval; a submission, passing check, or agent recommendation is not approval.

Any future opening of contribution intake requires separate human authorization of its scope and applicable license terms under [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). Existing code and documentation license boundaries remain in force; this document grants no rights in private Core or excluded material.

## Conduct and privacy

Be respectful. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. Confidential conduct complaints may be sent to the same private mailbox listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); Hakan handles these complaints.

Do not submit secrets, private records, or unnecessary personal data. Public account names, discussions, and commit metadata may persist in forks and platform history; complete deletion of public copies cannot be guaranteed.

Community participation in future research and governance is a planned direction, not an authority grant, intake promise, or permission to change the Constitution or private projects. Any later process must follow the [canonical Constitution](LEONARDO_TALES_CONSTITUTION.md), [Project Boundary](PROJECT_BOUNDARY.md), and [Human Approval Gate](HUMAN_APPROVAL_GATE.md).
